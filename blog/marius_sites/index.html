HappOut (or OutFayz)

An appp that uses ai to plan a trip of getting out with someone for you. Was last unity app in active development, but never got released.

The first ideas about it were shared in 4 november 2024. Originally called TrippyPlanner.

The app was to use a combination of OpenAI's ChtGPT and Google Maps in order to determine whats the best way to spend your day/days out.

WaterChecker (June 2025)

WaterChecker is an app made for a teacher that manages water consumption per a number of apartments.

The first mention of this program was made on 4th June 2025. The finished product was given to me on 7th June.
